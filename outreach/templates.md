## First email (day 0)

Subject: 2-4 lowercase words from the niche line below.

    Hi {Business} team,

    {NICHE LINE}

    We build AI assistants that answer on your website and phone 24/7, in a natural voice, answer common questions and
    take {booking word} requests, then pass them to your team. You can try ours right now, by chat or voice, at
    https://samplewebsite.tech. It's the same thing we'd set up for {Business}.

    Worth a quick look?

If the chatbot check was `unknown`, add before "Worth a quick look?":

    If you already have something like this, no worries, just ignore me.

### Niche lines and subjects
| Niche | Subject | Niche line (adapt with a real detail) |
|---|---|---|
| dentist | after-hours patients | When someone in {City} gets a toothache at 9pm and finds you, they can call or fill in a form and wait until morning. Plenty of them book with whoever answers first. |
| med spa | evening enquiries | Most med spa enquiries come in the evening, after people scroll Instagram. If nobody answers price and availability questions until the next day, many of them don't come back. |
| personal injury lawyer | after-hours intake | Accident victims usually reach out the same day, often late at night. The firm that responds first tends to sign the case. |
| plumber | missed emergency calls | Burst pipes don't wait for office hours. When you're on a job and can't pick up, that caller usually rings the next plumber on Google. |
| HVAC | calls while on jobs | When the AC dies in the heat, people call until someone answers. If your team is out on jobs, those calls go to voicemail and often to a competitor. |
| real estate agent | weekend buyer questions | Buyers browse listings at night and on weekends and want answers straight away: is it still available, can I view it Saturday. |
| chiropractor | new patient calls | New patients often call while you're with someone else. If they reach voicemail, a lot of them just try the next clinic. |
| veterinarian | worried pet owners | Pet owners with a worried question at night either wait, or call whoever answers. Most questions are simple: hours, prices, can I bring her in tomorrow. |
| auto repair | quote requests | People want a rough price and a slot before they drive in. If they can't get an answer quickly, they ring the next shop. |
| home cleaning | quote requests | Cleaning customers usually ask for a quote from two or three companies at once and go with the first clear answer. |

Booking word: appointment (dentist, med spa, chiropractor, vet), consultation (lawyer), job (plumber, HVAC,
cleaning, auto), viewing (real estate).
