Most goalkeepers stop the ball with their hands. This one... used his heels. Upside down.
September sixth, nineteen ninety-five. Wembley. England against Colombia. Just a friendly. What could possibly go wrong?
In goal for Colombia: René Higuita. His nickname? El Loco. The madman.
Jamie Redknapp tries a lob from thirty yards. A soft, easy ball. Any keeper would just catch it.
Not Higuita. He dives forward... flips his legs over his head... and kicks it away with his heels.
Twenty thousand people at Wembley had never seen anything like it.
And the funniest part? The flag was already up for offside. He didn't even need to save it.
It wasn't luck, either. He later said he'd practised it... all through the warm-up.
The match ended nil nil. Nobody remembers the score.
