In four minutes, one man scored the most famous handball in World Cup history... and the greatest goal.
June twenty-second, nineteen eighty-six. Mexico City. Argentina against England, in the quarter-final.
Fifty-one minutes. A looping ball drops towards England's goalkeeper. Diego Maradona jumps... and punches it in with his hand.
The goal stands. Maradona later said it was scored... "a little with the hand of God."
Four minutes later, he picks up the ball in his own half. He turns... and runs. Past one England player. Then another. And another. Around the goalkeeper... and in.
In two thousand two, a FIFA online poll voted it the Goal of the Century.
Argentina won two one... and went on to win the World Cup.
