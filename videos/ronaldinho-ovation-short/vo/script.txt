Real Madrid fans... standing up to applaud a Barcelona player. In their own stadium.
November nineteenth, two thousand five. The Clasico, at the Bernabeu. Barcelona were already one nil up.
Then Ronaldinho picked up the ball on the left... ran at Sergio Ramos... went past him... and fired it in. Two nil.
Eighteen minutes later, he did it again. Past Ramos... and low, under Iker Casillas. Three nil.
And then something strange happened. Thousands of Madrid fans rose to their feet... and clapped.
Before him, the only Barcelona player said to get that honour was Diego Maradona, in nineteen eighty-three.
Nine days later, Ronaldinho won the Ballon d'Or.
