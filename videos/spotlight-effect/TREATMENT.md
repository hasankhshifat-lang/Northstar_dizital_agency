# Spotlight Effect — treatment (three-style test)

Three 9:16 Shorts (1080×1920) about one idea with one narration, rendered in Marker on Paper, Whiteboard Explainer
and Stick-figure Explainer. The storyboard tables are in `social/scripts/spotlight-effect-short.md`; this file adds the
director's choices. Length: **11.2 s**. The voice is 10.2 s, and the answer is held 1.5 s after the last word. Silent
text versions are not made.

## Facts (FACTS.md has sources)
Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000), JPSP 78(2) 211–222. Students wearing an embarrassing T-shirt guessed about half the
room (≈46–50 %) had noticed it; about 23 % had. On-screen numbers are rounded the same way as the voice
("about half", "about 1 in 4"). The 12 people drawn are an illustration, not the study's head count.

## Candidate structures (shared across the three styles)
1. **Guess vs reality on one picture**: a room is drawn, the guess is marked, then the real number replaces it. *Chosen*:
   the payoff is a visible gap between two marks on the same people, which reads in one glance on a phone.
2. A countdown of heads turning away one by one until a quarter is left: good motion, but the "half" guess has nowhere to live.
3. A split screen of "in your head" and "in the room": clear, but it halves the picture size on a phone.

## Logline and arc
A student is sure the whole room saw the shirt; the room says otherwise.
Setup (the worry) → turn ("Real number?") → ending (only one in four looked, and the student straightens up).

## Benchmark (grammar only)
One-idea-per-drawing marker explainers and RSA-style whiteboard talks. Learn: one question per frame, the simplest
geometry, colour carrying meaning, writing that lands on the word. Don't take: characters, layouts, music, typefaces.

## Sound design (all three)
| Section | Ambience | Foley | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | none (a near-silence under the hook) | marker strokes / pops on each mark | none |
| B2–B3 | a faint room tone | steps, magnet clicks, strokes | a soft pluck pattern, 100 bpm, entering at B2 |
| B4 | the music drops out (the second silence) | one scratch / wipe | — |
| B5 | room tone | one reveal hit on "four" | the pluck resolves on the tonic and rings out |

## Camera per film (at least four moves each, every one motivated)
- **Marker**: pull back (hook scale) → peel (new sheet) → follow the pen (the path in) → lean in (the guess) → pull back (the comparison) → lean in (the red answer).
- **Whiteboard**: pull back from a tiny drawing → ride the line to the room region → hold → whip to the eraser → hold with a slow dolly-in.
- **Stick**: locked → pull back to the room → push in on the student → pull back → locked verdict.

## Signature moment (one per film)
Marker: the red "¼" as the last stroke on the sheet. Whiteboard: the eraser wiping the blue guess down to a ghost,
so guess and reality sit on one board. Stick: three heads turning toward the student and changing to the accent colour,
then the student straightening up.
