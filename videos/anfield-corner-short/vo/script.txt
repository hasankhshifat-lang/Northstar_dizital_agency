Barcelona lost a Champions League semi-final... because they weren't watching a corner.
May seventh, two thousand nineteen. Anfield. Liverpool had lost the first leg three nil. And Mohamed Salah and Roberto Firmino were both missing.
Divock Origi scored after seven minutes. Then, early in the second half, Georginio Wijnaldum scored twice in two minutes. Three all on aggregate.
Seventy-nine minutes. Liverpool win a corner. Trent Alexander-Arnold puts the ball down... and starts to walk away.
Barcelona's players turn their backs. They're still getting organised.
Then he spins round and whips it in. Origi is unmarked... and fires it in first time. Four nil.
Liverpool were in the final. Less than four weeks later, they won it.
