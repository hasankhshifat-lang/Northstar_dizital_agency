Germany scored four goals in six minutes... against Brazil. In Brazil.
July eighth, two thousand fourteen. A World Cup semi-final in Belo Horizonte. Brazil were without Neymar, injured. And their captain, Thiago Silva, was suspended.
Eleven minutes. Thomas Müller, unmarked from a corner. One nil.
Twenty-three minutes. Miroslav Klose. His sixteenth World Cup goal... a new record, passing Brazil's Ronaldo.
Then Toni Kroos scores. And again. Then Sami Khedira. Five nil... after twenty-nine minutes.
André Schürrle added two more. Oscar got one back at the end. Seven one.
Five days later, Germany won the World Cup.
