## Concepts (thumbnail-rules §12, decided before production, A3)
1. **Face + evidence (chosen):** Custer cut out large + the Herald line "could not believe it" ringed + the Tribune
   "Custer killed / 315" clipping. Long headline **"Could not believe it"** (4 words, Sheridan's printed words) ·
   Short **"Is it true?"** (3 words, Sherman's printed words). Neither repeats a title; both add the disbelief.
2. Person-of-doubt: Sheridan engraving + the same clipping, "Too terrible to be true?" (weaker: Sheridan's face is
   less recognisable than Custer's).
3. Object/location: Tribune front page "315" ringed + Morrow's battlefield photo, "Two days late" (weaker: no face).
