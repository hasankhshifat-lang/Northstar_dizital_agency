Two days after the battleship Maine blew up in Havana harbor in February 1898, Hearst's New York Evening Journal told its readers that government divers had found an eight-inch "torpedo hole" in her plates: "Spanish treachery." Lower on the same front page, the paper said the divers would go down "to-day." This is the story of that hole, the two Navy boards that put a mine in two different places, the war that followed in ten weeks, and the 1976 Navy study that found no evidence of a mine at all. Told from the real pages, February 1898 – December 1911.
