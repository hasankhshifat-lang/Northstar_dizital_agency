## Statements that are inferences (worded as such)
- "on the very page that announced the hole, the Navy's divers had not yet gone down": from the same page ("divers
  will go down to-day") and Rickover (Navy divers arrived 19 Feb). The film does not claim nobody had looked at the
  wreck before; Spanish boats and officers were at the wreck (Rickover ch. 5).
- "no later report ever confirmed it" / "the court's findings did not mention it": the findings summary (Age-Herald,
  Sun) and the 1911 board summary name a bent keel / bottom plating at frames 18 and 28–31, not an 8-inch hole.
- "the country was in no mood to wait": supported by the Journal's "Spanish treachery" page (17 Feb), the Age-Herald
  "cowardly message" headline (29 Mar) and the Barre "Remember the Maine" procession (25 Apr).
- "Ten weeks later, America and Spain were at war": 15 Feb → 25 Apr 1898.
