Disagreement noted and left out of the narration: the second table in the same issue (p. 2034, "Number of Deaths from
Influenza and Pneumonia … for Corresponding Weeks, After the Beginning of the 1918 Epidemic", checked against Census
figures) gives Philadelphia 706 / 2,637 / 4,597 / 3,021 / 1,203 / 375 / 164 by week of the epidemic, with the peak at
4,597. Its week alignment differs from the dated table; both put the peak week after 9 October. The film uses the
dated table (p. 2032) only.

Left out on purpose: claims that the parade "caused" the outbreak (historians link it, but it can't be proved from
these sources; the film says only that the crowd was large and gives the dates); Krusen's motives; the 7 Oct "tide has
turned … panic is over" quote on p. 2 (the speaker is a Board of Health official whose city is unclear in the scan).
