"Grip decreasing; danger passing, doctors believe." Philadelphia's Evening Public Ledger printed that on September 30, 1918, two days after the Liberty Loan parade on Broad Street. Over the next ten days the same paper said the flu had reached its peak, that the city was "over top", that it had reached high tide. Then it printed the worst week of deaths the city had ever recorded. A U.S. Public Health Service table, printed weeks later, shows when the real peak came. Told from the real pages, September – November 1918.
