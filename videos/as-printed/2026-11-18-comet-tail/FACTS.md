## Statements in the narration that are inferences (worded as such)
- "In America, that meant the night of Wednesday, May eighteenth": Flammarion's 2 a.m. May 19 is French time; US papers
  give Wednesday night, May 18 (Detroit Times, 19 May; Washington Herald, 9 Feb: "on May 18"; Coeur d'Alene: "8:35 o'clock last night").
- "Did Flammarion ever say it? These pages do not settle that.": we found no Flammarion text with the "snuff out" /
  "wholesale extinction" claim in these pages; Pickering (Washington Herald) doubted the quote. The film doesn't say he never said it.
- "most Americans were not hiding … They were looking up": supported by the comet-party and street-telescope coverage
  (Detroit Times 19 May; Harper's cover 21 May; Gate City 17 May "Comet parties are being organized"); stated as the film's reading.

## Not stated (no source in hand)
- Any death toll, suicides or "gas masks" (popular 1910 legends we could not source in these pages).
- Halley's later returns (1986, 2061), Mark Twain's comet remark.
