On Monday, 15 April 1912, American newspapers told readers the Titanic was afloat, under tow, and that everyone was saved. By then she had been on the sea floor for hours. We put Monday's front pages next to Tuesday's corrections and the National Archives record, and follow how the story went from "no lives lost" to more than fifteen hundred dead.
