On Thursday, 7 November 1918, American newspapers told their readers the First World War was over: "WAR IS OVER!", "ARMISTICE IS SIGNED", "HOSTILITIES CEASE AT ONCE". By that afternoon the State Department had denied it, and the real armistice was signed four days later. We read the front pages as they were printed, find the doubt that was on the same pages, and follow the bulletin back to an American admiral's office in Brest.
