Wording choices: the film says the Associated Press was the United Press's "great rival" (the Oklahoma City Times and
El Paso Herald ran AP; the Omaha Bee's hoax story is "By Associated Press"). It does not give casualty figures for 7–11 Nov,
the hour the bulletin reached New York, or the $85,000 clean-up figure (single secondary source). The "pause in the guns"
line is the Omaha Bee's own headline, offered as a clue, not as the proven cause. Rejected detail: the Seattle Star of
11 Nov gives the signing as 5:40 a.m.; House's telegram says 5 o'clock, and the film uses House.
