In July 1920 a New York front page called Charles Ponzi "the financial wizard", said to have made more than $9,000,000 in a few months from international postal reply coupons. Under the same headline, on page three, the Boston postmaster gave a very different number. This is the story of the original Ponzi scheme as the papers printed it: the 50 per cent promise, the crowds in Pie Alley, the run he kept paying, the arrest, and the guilty plea. Told from the real pages, July – November 1920.
