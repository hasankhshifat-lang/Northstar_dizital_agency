# FACTS — "No Lives Lost" (the Titanic, April 1912)

Every number and quotation on screen or in the narration. Retrieved 2026-11-14.

| # | Claim in the film | Source | Wording quoted |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | On the evening of Monday 15 April 1912 the *New Haven Union* printed "Titanic afloat, no lives lost" | *The New Haven Union* (New Haven, Conn.), City Edition, 15 April 1912, p. 1 — Library of Congress, Chronicling America, https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn92051126/1912-04-15/ed-1/?sp=1 | Masthead: "NEW HAVEN, CONN., MONDAY EVENING, APRIL 15, 1912." Headline: "TITANIC AFLOAT, NO LIVES LOST, SHIPS TO RESCUE" |
| F2 | The next morning the *New-York Tribune* printed 1,340 dead | *New-York Tribune*, 16 April 1912, p. 1 — LoC, https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn83030214/1912-04-16/ed-1/?sp=1 | "1,340 PERISH AS TITANIC SINKS; ONLY 886, MOSTLY WOMEN AND CHILDREN, RESCUED" |
| F3 | The same day the *Tribune* reported crowds at the White Star office | same page, left column | "CROWD BESIEGES WHITE STAR OFFICE" |
| F4 | The *Medford Mail Tribune* printed 1,492 dead on 16 April | *Medford Mail Tribune* (Medford, Ore.), First Edition, 16 April 1912, p. 1 — LoC, https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn97071090/1912-04-16/ed-1/?sp=1 | "1492 LIVES LOST IN TITANIC DISASTER" |
| F5 | The ship had already sunk before dawn on 15 April | National Archives, *Prologue* (Spring 2012), "Titanic" — https://www.archives.gov/publications/prologue/2012/spring/titanic.html | "sank less than three hours after the crew spotted an iceberg at 11:40 p.m. on April 14." |
| F6 | The Carpathia picked up 706 people from the lifeboats | NARA *Prologue*, same | "the 706 persons it picked up from Titanic's lifeboats on the morning of April 15, 1912" |
| F7 | 2,223 aboard, 1,517 died | NARA *Prologue*, same | "Of the 2,223 people aboard, 1,517 perished." |

Rights: the newspapers (1912) are in the public domain in the US. The Bain negatives are marked by the
Library of Congress "No known restrictions on publication" (https://www.loc.gov/rr/print/res/274_bain.html);
they were made and published in 1912, before 1931, so they are in the US public domain. Not CC0 as a licence label,
so this was named to the user.

Not used: other death tolls (the British inquiry's 1,503; the 1,492 United Press figure is shown only as the *Medford*
headline, not as a fact). The film states the NARA figure (F7) as the count.
