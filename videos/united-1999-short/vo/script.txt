Ninety minutes gone... and Manchester United were losing the Champions League final.
Bayern Munich had scored in the sixth minute, from a free kick. For more than eighty minutes, United couldn't find a way back.
The referee showed three minutes of added time. Bayern's ribbons were already being tied to the trophy. The UEFA president left his seat to go and present it.
Ninety-one minutes. A corner... and even the goalkeeper, Peter Schmeichel, runs up into the box. The ball breaks to Teddy Sheringham... one all.
Barely two minutes later... another corner. Sheringham flicks it on... and Ole Gunnar Solskjaer pokes it into the roof of the net. Two one.
Bayern's players collapsed on the grass. United had won their first European Cup in thirty-one years... and the treble.
Greatest finish to a final? Or can you name a better one?
