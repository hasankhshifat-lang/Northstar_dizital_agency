Barcelona needed three goals... in seven minutes.
They'd lost the first leg four-nil. No team had ever come back from that... and P-S-G had scored an away goal at the Camp Nou.
Eighty-eight minutes. Neymar... free kick... top corner.
Ninety-one. Penalty... Neymar again. Five-five on aggregate... but P-S-G still go through, on away goals.
One more goal. Final seconds... Neymar lifts it into the box... and substitute Sergi Roberto stretches out a foot.
Six-one. The celebrations were so loud, seismographs half a kilometer away recorded a small tremor.
Greatest comeback ever... or not even close?
