Brazil only needed a draw... to win the World Cup. At home.
July sixteenth, nineteen fifty. The Maracana, in Rio. Around two hundred thousand people, by most accounts.
There was no final that year, just a group of four. Brazil had beaten Sweden seven one, and Spain six one. Some newspapers were already calling them champions.
Just after half-time, Friaca scored. One nil Brazil.
Then Juan Schiaffino equalised for Uruguay. One all... still enough for Brazil.
With eleven minutes left, Alcides Ghiggia ran down the right... and shot low, inside the near post. Two one Uruguay.
The whole stadium... went silent.
Uruguay were world champions. Brazil's goalkeeper, Moacir Barbosa, was blamed for the rest of his life.
