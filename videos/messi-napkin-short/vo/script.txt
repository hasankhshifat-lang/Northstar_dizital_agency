Lionel Messi's first Barcelona contract... was written on a paper napkin.
As a boy in Rosario, Argentina, he was always the smallest player on the pitch. At around eleven, doctors found his body wasn't making enough growth hormone.
The treatment meant injections, night after night... reportedly about a thousand dollars a month.
At thirteen, he flew to Barcelona for a trial. The coaches were impressed... but the club kept stalling.
His family threatened to take him home. So on December fourteenth, two thousand, director Carles Rexach grabbed the nearest piece of paper... a napkin... and wrote down a promise to sign him.
Barcelona paid for his treatment.
That small boy went on to win eight Ballon d'Or awards... and the World Cup.
And the napkin? In twenty twenty-four, it sold at auction for nearly a million dollars.
Best signing ever... or can you name a better one?
