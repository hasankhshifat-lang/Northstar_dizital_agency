Bookmakers gave Leicester City a five thousand to one chance... of winning the Premier League.
A year earlier, they'd spent Christmas bottom of the table... and only just escaped relegation.
Then they hired Claudio Ranieri. Most predictions still had them going down.
But Jamie Vardy scored in eleven Premier League games in a row... a new record.
Riyad Mahrez danced past defenders. N'Golo Kanté won the ball back everywhere.
By Christmas... Leicester were top.
May the second, twenty sixteen. The players gathered at Vardy's house to watch Chelsea against Tottenham. Spurs had to win.
Two-nil Tottenham at half-time... then Chelsea fought back. Eden Hazard... two-two.
Leicester were champions, for the first time in their one hundred and thirty-two year history.
Biggest shock in sport... or can you name a bigger one?
