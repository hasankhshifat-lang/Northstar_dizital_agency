It was the last game of his career... a World Cup final. And Zinedine Zidane walked off with a red card.
July ninth, two thousand six. Berlin. France against Italy.
Seven minutes in, Zidane takes a penalty... and chips it. It hits the crossbar... bounces down... over the line.
Twelve minutes later, Marco Materazzi heads Italy level. One one.
Extra time. Zidane and Materazzi exchange words. Zidane walks away... then turns around... and headbutts him in the chest.
The referee didn't see it. The fourth official did.
Red card. Zidane walks past the World Cup trophy... and off the pitch, forever.
Italy won on penalties. And Zidane was still voted the best player of the tournament.
Materazzi later said he insulted Zidane's sister. Zidane never repeated the words.
