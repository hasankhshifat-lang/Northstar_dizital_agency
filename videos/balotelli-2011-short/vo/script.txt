Two nights before the Manchester derby... Mario Balotelli's bathroom caught fire. Fireworks.
October, twenty eleven. Just after midnight, a firework set the bathroom of his rented house alight.
Two fire engines came. Nobody was hurt. The repair bill? Reportedly... four hundred thousand pounds. And Mario? He blamed a friend.
Then, on Sunday... Manchester City went to Old Trafford.
Twenty-two minutes in, Balotelli scores. And he lifts his shirt. Underneath, a message: Why always me?
He scored again after the break. City won... six goals to one. At Old Trafford. United's worst home defeat in fifty-six years.
Sir Alex Ferguson called it... our worst ever day.
And the funniest part? Days later, Mario became the face of a firework safety campaign.
