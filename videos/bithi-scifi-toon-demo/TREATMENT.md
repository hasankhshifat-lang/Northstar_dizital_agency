# Treatment: Scientific Bithi in Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon (30 s demo, 9:16 Facebook Reel)

Brief (owner, 2026-11-16): a 30-second demo of the Scientific Bithi Shorts redrawn in the lemo-opuscar Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon
style. Keep the cast (মন্টু মামা, বিথী, বল্টু), keep a Bangladeshi setting, redraw the characters' animation in the
style (thick boiling line, flat fills with hard shadows, big white eyes, elastic mouths, acting on twos). Funny
dialogue, one science experiment with a correct explanation, and a scientist cameo through the portal, as in the
current Shorts. Language: Bangla (voices: ElevenLabs eleven_v4, the channel's fixed voices; never the library TTS).
Defaults I chose (not answered yet): 2D painted set in the style instead of a Blender plate (the style is flat 2D);
family-friendly jokes (the audience is NCTB class 6–10); portal in cyan + gold (never acid green); burned-in
Bangla subtitles above the Reel's bottom 400 px.

Topic: **do heavy things fall faster?** Mama bets the pumpkin will hit first; both land together. Newton explains
that the Earth gives everything the same acceleration when air doesn't get in the way.
(NCTB 9–10 physics: gravity/g, falling bodies; second source: Britannica "acceleration due to gravity".)
Setting: a Dhaka rooftop in the afternoon (water tank, parapet, clothesline with a gamchha, potted chilli plants,
skyline in flat colour bands). The drop is from a মোড়া (stool) into a tub of sand, so it is safe and visible.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Bet → splat → summon → explanation → deadpan button.** Mama bets, both land together (hard cut to an insert
   close-up of the tub, then reaction close-ups), Mama blames the pumpkin's shyness, Bithi summons Newton, portal,
   Newton explains over a slow-motion replay with equal arrows and strobe ticks, Mama asks about the apple on his
   head, Newton deadpans "it didn't fall on my head". Uses: duo contrast, absurd insert → reaction, portal, dead-air
   reversal, callback. Fits 30 s.
2. **Rule of three.** Mama drops pumpkin/lemon, brick/slipper, watermelon/marble: always together, faster each time,
   then the summon. Funny but no room left for the explanation and the cameo in 30 s.
3. **Mid-disaster opening.** The portal is already open and Newton is stuck half-way through as the pumpkin falls.
   Strong hook but the summon pattern (Bithi doesn't know → calls him) can't happen, and the owner wants it.

**Chosen: 1.** It keeps every part the owner asked for and follows the Shorts' structure in 30 s.

Fact notes: equal acceleration (g ≈ 9.8 m/s²) for all bodies when air resistance is negligible (NCTB; Britannica).
A pumpkin and a lemon dropped ~1 m land together within a frame (air resistance negligible at these speeds).
The apple: the earliest account (William Stukeley's memoir, 1752) says Newton saw an apple fall in his garden;
nothing says it hit his head. So "মাথায় না… পাশে" is accurate, said as a joke, and not presented as a quote.

Sound: synthesized theremin + analog bass + square arpeggio + drum machine (the style's palette) with a light
Bangla touch on the end card (a dotara-like pluck). Music cut on the splat reveal and in the cold pause.
