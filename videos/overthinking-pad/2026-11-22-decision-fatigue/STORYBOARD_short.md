# Storyboard (short): Is Decision Fatigue Real?

Notebook doodle (channel engine, Sketchnote base). Per scene: purpose · what is drawn (draw order = the cue words in episode.js) · shot and why · SFX. Characters react (L.react) and props sit at the hand positions person() returns.

## s1
> In twenty eleven, researchers looked at more than a thousand parole rulings by judges in Israel.

- **Purpose:** hook in the first second (study-first, EXP-001 control): a judge with a gavel mid-ruling, then the scale of the data
- **Shot:** opens close on the judge and gavel from frame 0, pulls back to reveal the stack of 1,000+ rulings (reveal)
- **SFX:** gavel bang on frame 0; paper flicks

## s2
> At the start of each session, about two out of three prisoners were granted parole. Just before the food break... almost none.

- **Purpose:** start of a session: 2 of 3 granted; just before the break: almost none
- **Shot:** pan right; the three figures at the top, the slope drawn down to a near-empty bottom row
- **SFX:** two cheerful plucks, then a descending pluck

## s3
> After the break... back up to two out of three. The internet called it decision fatigue. Hungry judges say no.

- **Purpose:** after the break: back to 2 of 3; the internet's story "hungry judges say no"
- **Shot:** pan right; the full saw chart, then the meme tag stamped over it; push on the tag
- **SFX:** upward pluck; a stamp

## s4
> But then other researchers checked. The cases weren't in random order. Prisoners without a lawyer were often heard last. And simulations showed that simply not starting a long case before the break could produce much of the drop.

- **Purpose:** the check: cases not random (no lawyer → last); not starting a long case before the break can make much of the drop
- **Shot:** pan right; magnifier sweeps a queue; the last two have no lawyer; then the clock and a thick file pushed aside
- **SFX:** magnifier swoosh; clock ticks; a file slide

## s5
> Tired judges may still be real. But the famous number is probably far too big.

- **Purpose:** the honest verdict: tired judges may be real; the famous number is probably far too big
- **Shot:** pan right; a scale tips: a small "real fatigue?" weight vs the huge famous number
- **SFX:** scale creak

## s6
> So... do you make your big decisions in the morning, or at night?

- **Purpose:** loop back to the judge + one comment question (morning or night?)
- **Shot:** pan right; the judge returns small at the top; sun vs moon; hold on the question
- **SFX:** pen; closing kalimba
