# Thumbnails: 2026-11-17 replay loop

Made with the universal thumbnail skill (`style: "notebook"`). Art = the channel's own doodle character, drawn by the
channel engine (`thumb_art/thumb_episode.js`, rendered with the engine page, no text in the art); composition, headline and tag in
**Remotion** (`render.mjs`, props `thumb_long.props.json` / `thumb_short.props.json`). No generated or third-party
images, so no Figma/Spline generations were needed (0 of 3 used). Mobile check viewed (`out/<name>_check.png`).
