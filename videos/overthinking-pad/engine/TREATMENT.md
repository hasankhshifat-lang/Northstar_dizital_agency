# Treatment: The Overthinking Pad channel template (Sketchnote base)

Written before opening the Sketchnote demo (lemo-opuscar AGENTS.md, "Treatment first").

## The film format

Every episode is **one long sheet of dot-grid notepad paper** that fills up as the episode goes. Each chapter owns a
region of the sheet; each scene is a "block" inside it (a doodle scene, a number, a diagram). The camera rides the pen
from block to block; chapters start with a whip across empty dots to a numbered yellow banner. The film ends on a
pull-back over the finished sheet, so every episode's last frame is a printable poster of what you learned.

Recurring cast: simple doodle people (round head, dot eyes with pupils that look somewhere, line mouth, bean body).
**"You"** is the one character with a yellow marker swash: the yellow thread of every episode follows the viewer's
own thought (the spotlight, the loop, the worry). Other people are black and grey. People react by the pen drawing
their next expression/pose beside or over the last (eyes re-drawn looking at something, a sweat drop, a laugh line),
never by sliding or bouncing.

## Three candidate structures (channel default)

A. **Question → experiment-as-story → twist → mechanism → what helps → payoff.** Each study is staged as a small
   doodle scene: setup, the guess (written as a number), the count (a marker bar). Best for research-led topics.
B. **Myth / fact split page:** the left half is "what it feels like", the right half "what the data shows". Strong but
   repetitive over 11 minutes.
C. **Timeline across the page:** studies in date order left to right. Clear but reads like a lecture; weak hook.

**Choice: A** as the channel default (episode file can reorder), with B used inside a chapter when useful (the
guess-vs-count bars are a mini split).

## Opening and ending (this episode)

- Open on the pen drawing "you" in a doorway in close-up, a yellow spotlight cone landing on them, room of eyes
  sketched around: the hook line is said over the first strokes (no logo first). Sting (≤ 2 s, the channel banner
  written by the pen) after the hook.
- End on the shirt again, then a pull-back to the whole sheet; the yellow cone is circled once. End card: banner
  "THE OVERTHINKING PAD" + "new pages Mon · Tue · Thu · Sat".

## Fixed look (channel identity)

Paper #FBFAF6, dots warm grey, ink #1E1E22, notes #5B5852, grey marker in three strengths, one yellow
rgba(255,205,40,.8) + dark yellow #F5AA00 for sparks. Hand lettering: Kalam Bold (titles, keywords), Patrick Hand
(notes). Subtitles on a paper slip with washi tape (16:9 bottom; 9:16 kept above the bottom 400 px). Music: small
plucked marimba/kalimba bed (procedural), pen foley from the strokes. Voice: VibeVoice Frank. Master -14 LUFS.
