# FACTS: The Spotlight Effect (2026-11-16)

Every claim in the narration and on screen, with its source. Abstracts were read from PubMed (NCBI E-utilities) and
Crossref. Full texts of the 2000 and 2002 papers were not reachable from this environment (web.archive.org blocked),
so study numbers that only appear in the full text are taken from consistent secondary summaries and spoken as
"about" / "close to".

## Labelled as examples / interpretation (not claims)

- "Imagine…" scenes (the coffee stain, the meeting) are hypothetical illustrations.
- "Everyone else is busy with their own spotlight" is presented as a reasonable reading of the anchoring explanation,
  not as a separate finding.
- The "count the room" exercise is our suggestion built on the research, framed as something to try, not a treatment.
