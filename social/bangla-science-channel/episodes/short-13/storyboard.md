## Checks on paper
- Hook in 1–3 s: absurd still image at 0 s, crumbs at 0.45 s, first line 4 words, Boltu at 2.0 s.
- Demonstration is accurate and visual: half the ball always lit (lantern side), the phase seen from Boltu's head changes as the ball goes round, the top-down orbit board with the matching phase inset; eclipse shown separately as Earth's shadow.
- No "stands and talks": every line has an action (biscuit, hiding, lantern, ball, turning, twirl, second bite).
- No needless movement: camera static except the point-of-view shot, where the movement is the demonstration.
