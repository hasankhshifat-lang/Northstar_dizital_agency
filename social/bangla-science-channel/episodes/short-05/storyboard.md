## Checks on paper
- Hook: the splash at 0.3 s is the first visual; the mystery is spoken by 4.8 s; no greeting.
- Demonstration: the ray goes from the fish (object) to the eye, bending away from the normal as it leaves the water (water side 29°, air side 40°, consistent with n = 1.33); the image sits on the back-extension of the ray that reaches the eye. 4 ft ÷ 1.33 ≈ 3 ft for viewing straight down, which the depth board says.
- The puzzle shows both options without marking the right one.
- No "stands and talks" scene: every talking shot has a reaction, a contact (hand on shoulder, chappal) or a board reveal.
- Only purposeful big moves: the hand plunges, Boltu's dive pose, Bithi's restraining hand, Mama raising the chappal.
