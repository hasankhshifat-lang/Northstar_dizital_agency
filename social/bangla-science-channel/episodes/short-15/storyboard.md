## Checks on paper
- Hook in 1–3 s: absurd still image at 0 s, shout at 0.3 s, the echo (sound + words) at ~1 s, cut to the hiding shot at 2.4 s.
- Demonstration is accurate and visual: one clap, its path to the wall and back, heard twice; the board compares a far and a near wall (the only thing that changes is the distance).
- No "stands and talks": every line has an action (helmet + broom, hiding, pointing, staff pose, the clap, the song, the notes).
- No needless movement: static camera, cuts only; the only motion graphics are the sound paths.
