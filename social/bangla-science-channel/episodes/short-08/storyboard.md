## Checks on paper
- Hook: the backwards word and the siren from frame 0; Boltu's first line is 4 words.
- No board lecture: both demonstrations use the scene's own objects (Boltu's sheet, the ambulance's word), 5–8 s each; the reveal happens in the real mirror.
- No "stands and talks": every talking shot has a gesture with a target (Mama's explanation, Bithi pointing at the mirror, Boltu holding the sheet up, Mama glaring at the mirror, the flex) or a walk.
- Accuracy: the mirror reflects the street behind the camera (the ambulance is there, never on the far side); in the reflection the lamps and the hand swap sides; the wording stays with the textbook ("ডান-বাম উল্টো দেখায়"); the puzzle card gives no answer.
- Rig limits: the sheet stays attached to Boltu's left hand (measured hand points), the close-ups show the hand gripping it; Boltu is short, so he reaches up to the mirror rather than standing in front of it.
