## Checks on paper
- Hook in 1–3 s: absurd image at 0 s, flake at 0.45 s, first line 6 words (learning DB: losses start at 2–3 s, so the first frame must already be odd).
- Demonstration is accurate and visual: the three-nail fair test (only the jar with BOTH water and air rusts), then the mechanism board.
- No "stands and talks": every line has an action (tongs, hiding the dao, tray, finger taps, bucket).
- No needless movement: camera moves only on "ছোঁয়াচে" (push-in) and the time-lapse; otherwise static.
