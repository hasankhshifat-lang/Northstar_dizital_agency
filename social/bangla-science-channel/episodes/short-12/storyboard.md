## Checks on paper
- Hook in 1–3 s: absurd still image at 0 s (Short 11's best-retaining pattern), bead at 0.45 s, first line 5 words, Boltu at 2.4 s.
- Demonstration is accurate and visual: pores under the magnifier, the cross-section with heat leaving the inside water, the kolshi-vs-jug thermometer comparison (no invented numbers), sweat evaporating.
- No "stands and talks": every line has an action (torch, pat, jug, magnifier, thermometers, tap, mug).
- No needless movement: camera push-in only on "ফ্রিজ"; everything else static or a cut.
