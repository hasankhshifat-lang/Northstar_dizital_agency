## Checks on paper
- Hook: the bottle dents at 0.2 s, before the first word; second visual beat (crunch) at 3.2 s.
- No board lecture: both demonstrations are cutaways of the scene's own objects (Boltu's glass, Mama's bottle), 7–8 s each.
- No "stands and talks": every talking shot has a held object, a test, a walk or a reaction.
- Accuracy: the arrows push on the open surface (glass) and on the walls (sealed bottle), never "pull" from the mouth; the bottle springs back only after air enters the hole; the puzzle card gives no answer.
- Rig limits: the bottle stays on the table and the long bendy straw goes to the mouth, so no fake arm-to-mouth motion; the pin poke is a forearm-only close-up.
