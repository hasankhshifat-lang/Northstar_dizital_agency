## Checks on paper
- Hook: lightning + a visible count in the first 3 s (the real phenomenon is the hook, no greeting).
- Demonstration: both light and sound start at the same instant at the bolt; only their speeds differ (no "light leaves first" misinformation). Speeds and the 3 s ≈ 1 km rule match NCTB class 9–10 physics (330 m/s, Table 7.01; light ≈ 3×10^8 m/s).
- No "stands and talks" scene: every talking scene has a reaction, a demonstration or an icon reveal. The only held prop is the paper bag (attached to Boltu's hands, never floating).
- No random moves: the only big body moves are the jolt at the thunder and the jump at the bag pop.
