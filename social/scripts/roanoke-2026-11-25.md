## Topic research report (scriptwriting rules 2.G)

- Story: in 1587 John White led about 115 English settlers (NPS: 117) to Roanoke Island. His granddaughter Virginia
  Dare was born there on 18 August 1587, the first English child born in the Americas. White sailed to England for
  supplies on 27 August 1587; the war with Spain (the Armada, a shipping ban, a looted 1588 attempt) kept him away
  until 1590. He landed on 18 August 1590, Virginia's third birthday, and found the houses taken down, a palisade,
  "CRO" carved on a tree and "CROATOAN" carved on a post, and no cross, the sign they had agreed on for danger.
  Croatoan was the island home of Manteo's people, friendly to the English. Weather (and, per Wikipedia, a snapped
  anchor cable) forced the ships to leave without searching it. What happened to the colonists is still unknown.
- Sources (agree on everything used): Wikipedia "Roanoke Colony"; National Geographic "The lost colony of Roanoke"
  (quotes White: CROATOAN "without any cross or sign of distress"); NPS Fort Raleigh "The Lost Colony", "1590 Voyage" /
  "1590 Rescue Voyage" (117 colonists, Maltese cross as the distress sign, weather).
- Left out or softened: exact number (115 vs 117 → "over a hundred"); the anchor cable (one source → "the weather
  turned, his ships had to leave"; the storm scene shows weather only); the distance to Croatoan (not in the sources
  read → "nearby"); modern archaeology and theories (disputed → "No one knows what happened").
- Demand: Roanoke is one of the best-known American mysteries; "one word carved into a post" is a strong loop.

## Script (139 words, as voiced)

Over a hundred English settlers vanished from this island... and left one word carved into a post.
What did it mean?
In fifteen eighty-seven, John White brought them to Roanoke Island, North Carolina. His granddaughter, Virginia Dare, was born there... the first English child born in the Americas.
Then White sailed home for supplies. War with Spain kept him away... for three years.
He finally landed... on Virginia's third birthday. The houses were gone.
On a tree, three letters. C, R, O. On a post, one word. Croatoan.
They had agreed: if you are in danger, carve a cross. There was no cross.
Croatoan was a friendly island nearby. But the weather turned, his ships had to leave, and he never searched it.
No one knows what happened... to the settlers who left one word carved into a post.
