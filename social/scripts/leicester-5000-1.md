## Script (Short, 9:16, VibeVoice Frank, numbers as words)

> **(0–4.8 s)** Bookmakers gave Leicester City a five thousand to one chance... of winning the Premier League.
> **(4.8–14 s)** A year earlier, they'd spent Christmas bottom of the table... and only just escaped relegation. Then they hired Claudio Ranieri. Most predictions still had them going down.
> **(14–26 s)** But Jamie Vardy scored in eleven Premier League games in a row... a new record. Riyad Mahrez danced past defenders. N'Golo Kanté won the ball back everywhere. By Christmas... Leicester were top.
> **(26–38.6 s)** May the second, twenty sixteen. The players gathered at Vardy's house to watch Chelsea against Tottenham. Spurs had to win. Two-nil Tottenham at half-time... then Chelsea fought back. Eden Hazard... two-two.
> **(38.6–48.5 s)** Leicester were champions, for the first time in their one hundred and thirty-two year history. Biggest shock in sport... or can you name a bigger one?
