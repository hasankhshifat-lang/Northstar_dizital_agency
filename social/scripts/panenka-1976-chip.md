## Script (VibeVoice Frank, numbers as words)

> The most important penalty of his life... and he chipped it down the middle.
> June twentieth, nineteen seventy-six. Belgrade. The European Championship final. Czechoslovakia against West Germany.
> Two two after extra time. For the first time, a European final goes to penalties.
> Seven kicks. Seven goals. Then... Uli Hoeness blasts West Germany's fourth... over the bar.
> Antonin Panenka steps up. Score this, and Czechoslovakia are champions.
> In goal: World Cup winner Sepp Maier.
> Panenka runs in... Maier dives to his left... and Panenka chips it, softly, into the middle.
> He practised it after training, betting his club's goalkeeper a beer or a chocolate bar on every kick.
> Today, that penalty has a name. A Panenka.
> Would you dare to take it? Tell me in the comments.
