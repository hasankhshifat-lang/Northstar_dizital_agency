# The US Army Fooled Hitler With Rubber Tanks (topic-queue #2, WR)
Slot: 2026-11-10 21:00 SGT (13:00 UTC, 08:00 ET). Narration 56.5 s, ~118 words.

These tanks are made of rubber. In 1944, America sent an army of artists to fool Hitler. So how do you fake an entire army?
Meet the Ghost Army. Eleven hundred soldiers: painters, designers, and radio men. Their tanks were inflatable. Ninety-three pounds each. A few men could carry one.
At night, they inflated a whole fake tank column, dragged tracks through the mud, and rolled in giant speakers, blasting recordings of engines and marching troops.
In March 1945, they pulled their biggest trick on the Rhine River. German eyes were drawn to the wrong place, while the real army crossed.
All told, about twenty deceptions. And the secret stayed classified for over fifty years, until 1996. In 2022, they were awarded the Congressional Gold Medal. These tanks were made of rubber.
