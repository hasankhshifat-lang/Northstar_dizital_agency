11. [photo · posq] A reply coupon was a small slip sold at post offices around the world. You mailed it abroad, and the person who got it could trade it for a stamp at their own post office. Ponzi's story was simple. After the war, money in Europe was weak. Buy coupons cheaply there. Cash them for more here.
12. [read · azrep] This usually takes forty five days, according to Ponzi, who says his profits have reached four hundred per cent in some transactions.
13. [table · azrep] Four hundred per cent. It sounded like a loophole that bankers had somehow missed. There was just one way to check it. Count the coupons.
