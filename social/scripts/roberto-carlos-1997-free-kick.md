## Script (Short, 9:16, VibeVoice Frank, numbers as words)

> This free kick bent so much... a ball boy ducked. And the goalkeeper didn't even move.
> June third, nineteen ninety-seven. Lyon, France. Brazil against France.
> Twenty-one minutes. A free kick, almost forty yards from goal. Roberto Carlos walks back... eighteen steps.
> He runs in and hits it with the outside of his left foot. The ball flies past the wall... so wide that a ball boy, way off to the right, ducks.
> Then it swerves. Back towards the goal... off the post... and in.
> The goalkeeper, Fabien Barthez, never moved.
> For years, people called it a fluke. In twenty ten, French physicists explained it: hit hard enough, from far enough, a spinning ball curls more and more... in a spiral.
> So, luck... or physics? Tell me in the comments.
