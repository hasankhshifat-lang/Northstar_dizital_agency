## Topic research report (scriptwriting rules 2.G)

- Story: the marathon at the 1904 St. Louis Olympics (30 August 1904) started at about 90 °F on dusty roads, with one
  water stop at mile 12; 32 runners started. Fred Lorz got cramps after about nine miles and rode in a car, then got out,
  jogged into the stadium and was hailed as the winner until spectators revealed it; he was disqualified (he said it
  was a joke). Thomas Hicks, fading, was given strychnine (a rat poison) mixed with egg whites by his trainers, later
  with brandy, and won (3:28:53). Cuban runner Andarín (Félix) Carvajal ate rotten apples from an orchard, got cramps and
  took a nap; a wild dog chased Len Tau (Taunyane) about a mile off course.
- Sources (agree on everything used): Wikipedia "Athletics at the 1904 Summer Olympics – Men's marathon"; Smithsonian
  Magazine, "The 1904 Olympic Marathon May Have Been the Strangest Ever" (2012).
- Left out because only one source has it: 14 finishers, the car breaking down at mile 19, Alice Roosevelt's wreath, Hicks
  being held up across the line, Carvajal's 4th place, William Garcia's hemorrhage (details differ).
- Demand: Olympics + "weird but true" + a villain twist; the 1904 marathon is a proven viral history topic (many 1M+
  Shorts), not yet on this channel.
