## Script (Short, 9:16, VibeVoice Frank, numbers as words)

> **(0–4.9 s)** Lionel Messi's first Barcelona contract... was written on a paper napkin.
> **(4.9–14 s)** As a boy in Rosario, Argentina, he was always the smallest player on the pitch. At around eleven, doctors found his body wasn't making enough growth hormone.
> **(14–28 s)** The treatment meant injections, night after night... reportedly about a thousand dollars a month. At thirteen, he flew to Barcelona for a trial. The coaches were impressed... but the club kept stalling. His family threatened to take him home.
> **(28–39 s)** So on December fourteenth, two thousand, director Carles Rexach grabbed the nearest piece of paper... a napkin... and wrote down a promise to sign him. Barcelona paid for his treatment.
> **(39–55.7 s)** That small boy went on to win eight Ballon d'Or awards... and the World Cup. And the napkin? In twenty twenty-four, it sold at auction for nearly a million dollars. Best signing ever... or can you name a better one?
