## Script (VibeVoice, Frank; no audio tags, numbers as words)
1. The guards at Alcatraz checked these beds... and the heads were fake. Soap, toilet paper, and real hair. So where were the three men?
2. For months, Frank Morris and the Anglin brothers dug through the vents behind their sinks... with spoons.
3. They built a raft from more than fifty stolen raincoats.
4. On June eleventh, nineteen sixty-two, they climbed to the roof, crept down to the water... and paddled into the cold, dark bay.
5. A fourth man, Allen West, couldn't get out of his cell in time. He was left behind.
6. The searchers found a paddle... and pieces of a life vest. Never the men.
7. The FBI closed the case. They probably drowned. But the United States Marshals still keep it open.
8. All they left behind... were the heads in the beds. (loops to line 1)

Fact check (only what the sources agree on): escape on the night of 11 June 1962, discovered at the morning count on 12 June (FBI, NPS, Wikipedia); dummy heads of soap, toilet paper and real hair from the prison barbershop, painted, left in the beds (FBI, NPS, Wikipedia, HISTORY); months of widening the air vents under the cell sinks with spoons (and an improvised drill, left out) (FBI, NPS, Wikipedia); raft and life vests made from more than 50 stolen raincoats (FBI, Wikipedia, HISTORY); up through a utility corridor/vent to the roof, down to the northeast shore, launched into the bay (FBI, NPS, Wikipedia); Allen West could not get through his vent in time and stayed behind (FBI, NPS, Wikipedia); paddle and pieces of the raincoat life vests found in the bay (FBI, Wikipedia; NPS mentions debris); FBI closed its case on 31 Dec 1979, concluding they most likely drowned (FBI, Wikipedia); the US Marshals Service keeps the case open (USMS, FBI, Wikipedia). Left out: the guard touching a head and it falling (reported in some retellings; shown on screen only as staging of the discovery), the 2013 letter, the 1975 photo, survival theories.
