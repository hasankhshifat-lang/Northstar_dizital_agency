## What the winners have in common
1. **Fast cutting: a new shot every 1–4 seconds.** Nothing stays on screen long; the camera always moves (push-in, pan, parallax).
2. **A recurring scoreboard.** Every "how long would you last" winner shows a **survival tier list** ("Whole life → Years → Days → Hours → Minutes") and updates it after each era/job. It's the reason people keep watching: "where does the next one land?"
3. **One simple, expressive "you".** ONE RULE (closest to us) uses a very simple face whose **eyebrows, eyes and sweat drops** do all the acting. Their character is less detailed than Kit v2; the expression is what sells it.
4. **Big title cards per section** (era/job name, year) and **kinetic keywords** ("KILLED", "CHOLERA") that pop on screen with the narration.
5. **Contrast of mood by colour:** dull, desaturated past vs sharp reds/oranges for danger; cold blue vs warm fire.
6. **Shorts open on a visual shock in the first second** (a man in a coffee-tin outfit; your own hands in a cave) and never hold a static frame.

## How we compare (Kit v2 + Remotion)
- Character: Kit v2 already has what ONE RULE has (ink outlines, eyebrows, sweat drop) plus more detail and a signature colour. ✅
- **Shot length: ours is ~6–10 s per shot** — 2–4× slower than every winner. ❌ Biggest gap.
- **No scoreboard / survival timer.** ❌
- No kinetic keywords; captions only. ❌
- Backgrounds are clean flat vector; ONE RULE adds paper/paint texture and parallax depth. ⚠️

## What we'll build next (Remotion)
1. **Cut every 2–4 s:** split each segment into many more shots (close-up of Kit's face, insert of the threat, wide, reaction), each with a camera move.
2. **"HOW LONG WOULD YOU LAST?" board** — a recurring animated graphic, green → red, updated at the end of each section, plus an on-screen survival timer.
3. **Kinetic keywords** — 1–2 words per danger beat, popping in with a shake.
4. **Section title cards** with big era/year numbers.
5. **Texture + parallax:** a subtle paper/grain overlay and 3 depth layers (foreground grass/snow, Kit, background) moving at different speeds.
6. **Shorts:** first frame = Kit's scared face or the threat up close; captions 1–3 words; no shot longer than 2 s.
