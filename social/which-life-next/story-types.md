# Which Life Next — Short story types: what gets views

Every Short is tagged with ONE type. The daily run updates views (public counts via Clawlink youtube-data
`channel_shorts`, or vidIQ when it has credits) and picks the next day's 3 standalone stories:
**2 from the best type by average views at 24 h, 1 test from another type.** Re-rank weekly (Mondays) on 72 h views.
A type needs 3+ Shorts before it can lose its place; a test that beats the leader becomes a leader.

## Types
| Type | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Rescue / hero** | One person saves others against the odds | Minnie Freeman ties 12 kids together in a blizzard |
| **Sudden disaster** | Nature turns deadly in minutes/hours | 50°F to −27°F in one afternoon (1888) |
| **Deadly disease** | Illness kills fast, nobody knows why | Cholera on the Oregon Trail |
| **So close to safety** | Death or survival by inches | Found 12 feet from his own door |
| **Lost / stranded** | Trapped with no way out (mines, ice, desert, sea) | (to test) |
| **Outlaw / crime** | Robberies, shootouts, escapes | (to test) |
| **Weird but true** | Bizarre real history that sounds fake | (to test) |
| **Everyday hard life** | Prices, chores, rules of daily life | $300 for the Oregon Trail; July 4th deadline |
