## Description
In 1875, a locust swarm bigger than California crossed the Great Plains: an estimated 3.5 to 12.5 trillion Rocky Mountain locusts, the greatest concentration of animals ever recorded. A year earlier, in July 1874, the locust plague had stripped farms from Texas to the Dakotas in days. Could you have survived it?

You are Kit, a homesteader in Nebraska, in your second summer on the claim. The corn is waist high. Then a glittering cloud comes from the west.
