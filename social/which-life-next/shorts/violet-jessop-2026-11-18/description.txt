Violet Jessop was a stewardess on the Olympic when HMS Hawke rammed it in 1911, on the Titanic when it sank in 1912, and a nurse on the Britannic when it hit a mine in 1916. On the Titanic, she later wrote, an officer handed her a baby in the lifeboat; on the Britannic she jumped from a lifeboat drifting into the propellers and hit her head on the keel. She survived all three and lived to 83.
