She was on the Titanic when it sank... and on both of its sister ships when they were hit. How did one woman survive all three?

Violet Jessop was a ship's stewardess.

In nineteen eleven, she was aboard the Olympic when a British warship rammed it. It limped back to port.

Seven months later, she was on the Titanic. As it sank, she was ordered into a lifeboat... and, she later wrote, an officer handed her a baby.

On the rescue ship, a woman took the baby... without a word.

Then came World War One. Violet became a nurse on the third sister, the Britannic.

In nineteen sixteen, it hit a mine. Her lifeboat was pulled toward the spinning propellers. She jumped... was sucked under... and hit her head on the keel.

She survived that too. She went back to sea for decades... and died at eighty-three, on dry land.
