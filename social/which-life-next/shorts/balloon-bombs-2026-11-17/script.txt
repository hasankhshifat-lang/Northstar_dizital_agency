Six people on a church picnic in Oregon... were killed by a bomb from Japan. No plane ever came near. So how did it get there?

Japan built more than nine thousand balloons... out of paper, glued together by schoolgirls.

Each one carried bombs, and rode the jet stream across the Pacific Ocean... in about three days.

Hundreds landed in North America, from Alaska to Mexico.

But America kept it secret. Newspapers printed nothing... so Japan would think the balloons had failed.

Then, on May fifth, nineteen forty-five, near Bly, Oregon, Elsie Mitchell and five children found one in the woods, while her husband parked the car.

Someone pulled at it... and it exploded.

They were the only people killed by enemy attack on the American mainland in World War Two.

And Japan had already given up... believing every balloon had failed. Weeks before six people went on a picnic.
