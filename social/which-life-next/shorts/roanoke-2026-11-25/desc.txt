In 1587 John White brought more than a hundred English settlers to Roanoke Island, North Carolina, where his granddaughter Virginia Dare became the first English child born in the Americas. War with Spain kept him in England until 1590; when he returned on Virginia's third birthday, the houses were gone, "CRO" was carved on a tree and "CROATOAN" on a post, and there was no cross, the sign they had agreed on for danger. Bad weather forced his ships away before he could search Croatoan, and what happened to the colonists is still unknown.
