# This Chicken Lived 18 Months Without a Head (queue #3, WD)
Slot: 2026-11-11 21:00 SGT (13:00 UTC, 08:00 ET). Narration 54.4 s, ~125 words.

In 1945, a farmer chopped the head off this chicken. It kept walking. So how does a chicken live without a head?
Fruita, Colorado. September tenth. Lloyd Olsen swings his axe at a rooster for dinner. But the rooster gets back up... and walks away.
The axe missed the jugular vein, and left most of the brain stem. That part runs breathing, heartbeat, and balance. So the body kept going. Olsen fed him with an eyedropper: milk and water, straight into the throat.
They named him Mike. He toured America, and Life magazine ran his picture. Eighteen months later, in an Arizona motel, Mike choked. The syringe to clear his throat was missing.
