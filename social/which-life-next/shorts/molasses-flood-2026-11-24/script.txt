In nineteen nineteen, a wave of molasses tore through Boston... and killed twenty-one people.
How can syrup kill?
Boston's North End. A steel tank, fifty feet tall, held two point three million gallons of molasses. It leaked so badly, molasses dripped into the street.
January fifteenth was oddly warm. Just after noon... the tank burst open.
A brown wall of syrup rolled down Commercial Street at thirty-five miles an hour. It snapped the legs of the elevated railway. It swept up wagons, horses, and people.
Then the cold set in. The molasses thickened like glue... and held anyone it caught.
The company blamed a bomb. A court blamed the leaking tank that sent a wave of molasses through Boston.
