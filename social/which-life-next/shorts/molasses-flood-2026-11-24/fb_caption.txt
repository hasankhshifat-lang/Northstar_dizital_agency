A Wave of Molasses Killed 21 People

On January 15, 1919, a 50-foot tank holding 2.3 million gallons of molasses burst in Boston's North End. A brown wave rolled down Commercial Street at about 35 mph, snapped the elevated railway's supports, and killed 21 people as the molasses thickened in the cold.
