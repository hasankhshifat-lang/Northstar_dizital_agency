The winner of the nineteen oh four Olympic marathon... was fed rat poison during the race.
How did that happen?
Saint Louis. Ninety degrees. Thirty-two runners on dusty roads, with one water stop... at mile twelve.
Fred Lorz got cramps after nine miles, so he climbed into a car. Later he hopped out, jogged into the stadium, and was cheered as the winner... until people saw the trick.
Behind him, Thomas Hicks was fading. His trainers gave him strychnine, a rat poison, mixed with egg whites... and later, brandy.
Meanwhile, a Cuban runner ate rotten apples, got cramps, and took a nap. A wild dog chased another runner a mile off course.
