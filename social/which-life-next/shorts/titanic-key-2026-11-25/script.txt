This little key left the Titanic... the day before it sailed. And the men in the crow's nest had no binoculars.
Was this key the reason?
At Southampton, Officer David Blair was bumped off the ship... and went home with a crow's nest key in his pocket.
The lookouts had asked for binoculars. They were told... there were none for them.
April fourteenth, nineteen twelve. Twenty to midnight. Fred Fleet saw the iceberg with his bare eyes. He rang the bell... and phoned the bridge. Too late.
At the inquiry, he was asked how much sooner binoculars would have shown it. Fleet said: enough to get out of the way.
Did that key lock up the binoculars? Historians still argue.
In two thousand seven, it sold for ninety thousand pounds. The little key... that left the Titanic the day before it sailed.
