The Key That Left the Titanic Too Early

The day before the Titanic sailed, Officer David Blair was taken off the ship and went home with a crow's nest key. The lookouts had no binoculars, and Fred Fleet saw the iceberg with his naked eye. Historians still argue about what that key really locked.
