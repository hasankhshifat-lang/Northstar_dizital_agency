The day before the Titanic sailed in April 1912, Second Officer David Blair was taken off the ship at Southampton and went home with a key from the crow's nest. The lookouts had no binoculars, and Fred Fleet spotted the iceberg with his naked eye; at the US Senate inquiry he said binoculars would have shown it "enough to get out of the way." Historians still argue about what that key actually locked. It sold at auction in 2007 for £90,000.
