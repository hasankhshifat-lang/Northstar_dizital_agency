Blackbeard was the most feared pirate on the American coast, but there's no record he killed anyone before his very last fight. He ran on fear: fuses smoking under his hat, ribbons in his beard, six pistols across his chest. In 1718 he blockaded Charleston and his ransom was a chest of medicine. That November the Royal Navy caught him at Ocracoke Inlet, North Carolina.
