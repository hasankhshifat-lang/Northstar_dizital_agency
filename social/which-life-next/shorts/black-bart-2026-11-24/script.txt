For eight years, a polite bandit robbed stagecoaches in California... and left poems behind.
So how did they catch him?
He stepped into the road with a shotgun and a flour sack over his head, took the Wells Fargo strongbox, and walked away. He never shot anyone. Twenty-eight robberies.
Twice, he left a poem, signed... Black Bart, the P O eight.
Then, in eighteen eighty-three, near Copperopolis, a robbery went wrong. He ran... and dropped a handkerchief.
On it was a tiny laundry mark. F, X, O, seven.
Detectives checked about ninety laundries in San Francisco... until one knew the mark.
It belonged to Charles Boles, a well-dressed gentleman.
He got six years in San Quentin. After four, he walked out... and vanished, the way he had for eight years.
