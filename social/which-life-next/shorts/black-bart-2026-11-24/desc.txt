From 1875 to 1883 a polite bandit robbed at least 28 Wells Fargo stagecoaches in Northern California, wearing a flour sack over his head and never shooting anyone. Twice he left a poem signed "Black Bart, the PO8." After his last robbery near Copperopolis on November 3, 1883, he dropped a handkerchief with the laundry mark F.X.O.7; detectives checked about 90 San Francisco laundries until one traced it to Charles E. Boles. He got six years in San Quentin, was released after four, and vanished.
