On February 7, 1812, at about 3:45 in the morning, a huge earthquake hit New Madrid, a frontier town on the Mississippi. The riverbed lifted, waves raced upstream, boats were thrown onto the banks, and witnesses saw the Mississippi run backward. The quakes destroyed New Madrid and created Reelfoot Lake in Tennessee, and the New Madrid fault is still active today.
