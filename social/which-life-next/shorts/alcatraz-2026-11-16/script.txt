The guards at Alcatraz checked these beds... and the heads were fake. Soap, toilet paper, and real hair. So where were the three men?

For months, Frank Morris and the Anglin brothers dug through the vents behind their sinks... with spoons.

They built a raft from more than fifty stolen raincoats.

On June eleventh, nineteen sixty-two, they climbed to the roof, crept down to the water... and paddled into the cold, dark bay.

A fourth man, Allen West, couldn't get out of his cell in time. He was left behind.

The searchers found a paddle... and pieces of a life vest. Never the men.

The FBI closed the case. They probably drowned. But the United States Marshals still keep it open.

All they left behind... were the heads in the beds.
