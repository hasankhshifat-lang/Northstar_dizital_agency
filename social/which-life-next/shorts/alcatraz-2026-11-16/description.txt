On the morning of June 12, 1962, Alcatraz guards found dummy heads made of soap, toilet paper and real hair in three beds. Frank Morris and John and Clarence Anglin had dug through the vents behind their sinks with spoons, built a raft from more than 50 stolen raincoats and paddled into San Francisco Bay. A fourth man, Allen West, was left behind. The FBI closed its case in 1979, concluding they most likely drowned; the US Marshals Service still keeps it open.
