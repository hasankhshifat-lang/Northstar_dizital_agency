To drive to the Northwest Angle, a piece of Minnesota, you have to go through Canada. In 1783 the treaty that ended the Revolution drew the border with a 1755 map that got Lake of the Woods and the Mississippi wrong, and the 1818 fix (a line straight south to the 49th parallel) cut this corner off from the rest of the country. Today residents drive about 60 miles through Manitoba and check in with US border officers by video in a little shed.
