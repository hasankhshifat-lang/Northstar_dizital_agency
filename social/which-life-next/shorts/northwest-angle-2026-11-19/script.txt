To drive to this part of Minnesota... you have to go through Canada. Why is a piece of America stuck up there?
In seventeen eighty-three, after the Revolution, Britain and America drew a new border... using the best map they had.
The treaty said the line would run to the northwest corner of Lake of the Woods... then west, to the Mississippi.
But the map was wrong. The lake was a maze of bays... and the Mississippi started far to the south.
In eighteen eighteen, they patched it. From that corner, the line dropped straight south... to the forty-ninth parallel.
That one line cut off a piece of America.
It's the only part of the lower forty-eight north of that line. To drive home, people cross about sixty miles of Canada... then check in with border officers on a screen, in a little shed.
All because of one bad map.
