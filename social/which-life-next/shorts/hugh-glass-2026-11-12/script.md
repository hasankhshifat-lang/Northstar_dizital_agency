# Left for Dead, He Crawled 200 Miles (queue #4, WW)
Slot: 2026-11-12 21:00 SGT (13:00 UTC, 08:00 ET). Narration 58.2 s, 131 words. Video 58.7 s.

Hook (on screen from frame 0): LEFT FOR DEAD.

[tense] His own men left him for dead... and took his rifle. So how did he survive two hundred miles alone, to get it back?
August, 1823. South Dakota. Fur trapper Hugh Glass surprises a mother grizzly with two cubs. [tense] She mauls him. His neck. His back. A broken leg.
His captain asks two men to stay until he dies, then bury him. John Fitzgerald, and a young man, probably Jim Bridger. [tense] But they grab his rifle and knife... and leave.
[quietly] Glass wakes up alone. No gun. No food. Fort Kiowa is two hundred miles away. So he crawls. He eats berries, roots, and scavenged meat.
Six weeks later, he reaches the fort. He forgives the young man. Fitzgerald has joined the army... so Glass takes his rifle back. All because his own men left him for dead... (loops to line 1)
