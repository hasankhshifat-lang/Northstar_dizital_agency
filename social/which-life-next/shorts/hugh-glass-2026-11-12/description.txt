In August 1823, fur trapper Hugh Glass was mauled by a mother grizzly near the forks of the Grand River in today's South Dakota. The two men told to stay with him until he died, John Fitzgerald and a young man who was probably Jim Bridger, took his rifle and knife and left him. Glass survived and made it more than 200 miles to Fort Kiowa on the Missouri River in about six weeks, living on berries, roots and scavenged meat. He later forgave the young man and got his rifle back from Fitzgerald, who had joined the army.
