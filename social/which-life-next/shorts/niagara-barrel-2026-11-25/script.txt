A teacher in her sixties climbed into a barrel... and went over Niagara Falls. And she lived.
Why would she do that?
It was nineteen oh one. Annie Edson Taylor was a widow, out of money, who claimed to be in her forties.
Her plan to get rich: be the first person to go over the falls in a barrel, and survive.
She had a wooden barrel built, padded inside. Two days before, a cat went over first. It came out alive.
On October twenty-fourth, helpers sealed her in and set her adrift above the Horseshoe Falls.
She went over the edge... and about twenty minutes later, they pulled her out. Alive, with a cut on her head.
Her warning: nobody should ever try this again.
But the fortune never came. Her manager ran off with the barrel. She died poor... the teacher who went over Niagara Falls.
