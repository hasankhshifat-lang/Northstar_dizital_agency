On October 24, 1901, Annie Edson Taylor, a widowed schoolteacher in her sixties who told people she was in her forties, became the first person to go over Niagara Falls in a barrel and survive. Her padded wooden barrel was tested with a cat two days before; she was pulled out about twenty minutes after going over the Horseshoe Falls with only a cut on her head, and warned that no one should ever try it again. The fortune she hoped for never came: her manager ran off with the barrel, and she died poor in 1921.
