In May 1862, Robert Smalls, an enslaved wheelman on the Confederate steamer Planter, waited until the white officers went ashore, picked up his wife and children at another wharf, and steered the ship out of Charleston at night. Wearing the captain's straw hat and giving the right whistle signal, he passed Fort Sumter, then raised a white bedsheet and handed the ship to the Union Navy. He was later elected to Congress.
