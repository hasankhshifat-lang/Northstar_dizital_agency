In eighteen sixty-two, an enslaved man stole a Confederate warship... and sailed it past Fort Sumter. How did he get away with it?
Robert Smalls steered the Planter, an armed steamer in Charleston. One night in May, the white officers went ashore... and left the ship to the enslaved crew.
At three in the morning, Smalls fired the boilers. He stopped at another wharf... and picked up his wife and children.
He put on the captain's straw hat. From the fort, he looked like the captain.
At Fort Sumter, he gave the right whistle signal... and the fort let him pass.
A Union ship nearly fired... until its crew spotted a white bedsheet on the mast.
Smalls handed over the ship... and everyone aboard was free.
He was later elected to Congress... and bought the house where he was once enslaved.
It started the night he stole a Confederate warship.
