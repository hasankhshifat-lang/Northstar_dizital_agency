# NorthStar Dizital — brand profile & voice (Facebook)

Read before writing any caption, script or on-screen text. Marketing skills
(`caption-writer`, `hook-writer`, `copywriting`, …) ask for a brand profile and voice: this is it.

## Who we talk to
Owners of small local businesses in Bangladesh: parlour/salon, bridal studio, clinic/doctor,
restaurant, cleaning service, coaching centre, shop. Busy, practical, use Facebook and WhatsApp
every day, not technical, careful with money. They want more customers, not "digital transformation".

## Their problems (use one per post)
- Customers Google them and find nothing, or an old Facebook post.
- Enquiries come at night or while they're busy — and get missed.
- Facebook reach keeps dropping; they don't own the page's audience.
- Prices and services are asked again and again in inbox.
- Competitors look more professional online.
- Bookings are messy (calls, inbox, notebook).

## Voice fingerprint
- Talks like a friendly Dhaka tech person explaining over tea: short sentences, everyday Bangla.
- English for tech words, exactly as people say them: website, Google, search, WhatsApp, booking,
  AI, chatbot, page, post, click, online, design, mobile.
- Starts from the owner's situation ("আপনার…"), not from us.
- Light humour and a little emotion are fine (😬 🤔 👉); max 2–3 emoji per caption.
- Bangla digits in Bangla text (৳৮,০০০, ৩–৫ দিন).

## Never
- Formal/textbook Bangla ("ডিজিটাল উপস্থিতি নিশ্চিতকরণ"), forced translations of tech words.
- Hype words: "সেরা", "১০০% গ্যারান্টি", "revolutionary", "game-changer", "unlock", "elevate".
- Invented numbers, client names, reviews, ratings or results.
- More than one CTA. More than 2 hashtags.
- Calling demo/concept projects (doctor, cleaning) real clients.
