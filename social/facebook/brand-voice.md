# NorthStar Dizital — brand profile & voice (Facebook)

Read before writing any caption, script or on-screen text. Marketing skills
(`caption-writer`, `hook-writer`, `copywriting`, …) ask for a brand profile and voice: this is it.

## Who we talk to
Owners of businesses in Bangladesh, online and offline: f-commerce pages, shops and showrooms,
parlour/salon, clinic/doctor, pharmacy, restaurant, ISP/IT service, coaching centre, real estate,
hotel, travel agency. Busy, practical, use Facebook and WhatsApp
every day, not technical, careful with money. They want more customers, not "digital transformation".

## Main offer (from 2026-10-30)
AI assistant / AI receptionist for any business, online or offline: answers questions, sells
products and takes orders, books appointments, IT help and troubleshooting, product/service
queries, collects leads — 24/7, in Bangla/English/Banglish, on Messenger, WhatsApp, website,
Instagram and phone (voice). Less cost than a full-time staff member, never off sick, never
tired of the same question. Hands hard cases to a human. Websites are now a supporting service.
Pillars and angles: `content-pillars.md`.

## Their problems (use one per post)
- Messages come at night and get answered next morning — the customer is gone.
- Inbox fills up in Eid/sale season; staff can't keep up.
- The phone rings while everyone is busy.
- Staff leave, get sick, need training; owner ends up answering inbox all day.
- Same questions ("price কত?", "delivery কবে?") all day long.

Website-related pains (secondary, only as a side point):
- Customers Google them and find nothing, or an old Facebook post.
- Enquiries come at night or while they're busy — and get missed.
- Facebook reach keeps dropping; they don't own the page's audience.
- Prices and services are asked again and again in inbox.
- Competitors look more professional online.
- Bookings are messy (calls, inbox, notebook).

## Voice fingerprint
- Talks like a friendly Dhaka tech person explaining over tea: short sentences, everyday Bangla.
- English for tech words, exactly as people say them: website, Google, search, WhatsApp, booking,
  AI, chatbot, page, post, click, online, design, mobile.
- Starts from the owner's situation ("আপনার…"), not from us.
- Light humour and a little emotion are fine (😬 🤔 👉); max 2–3 emoji per caption.
- Bangla digits in Bangla text (৳৮,০০০, ৩–৫ দিন).

## Never
- Formal/textbook Bangla ("ডিজিটাল উপস্থিতি নিশ্চিতকরণ"), forced translations of tech words.
- Hype words: "সেরা", "১০০% গ্যারান্টি", "revolutionary", "game-changer", "unlock", "elevate".
- Invented numbers, client names, reviews, ratings or results.
- More than one CTA. More than 2 hashtags.
- Calling demo/concept projects (doctor, cleaning) real clients.

## Facts we can use
AI assistant service: no public price yet — never state one; invite them to WhatsApp for a
consultation instead. "Less cost than a staff salary" is fine as a general comparison; never
put a number on it or on results (no "৮০% বেশি sale").
