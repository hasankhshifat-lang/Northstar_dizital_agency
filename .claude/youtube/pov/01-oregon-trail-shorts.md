## Short A - Cholera (39s)

**Title:** POV: The Oregon Trail's Deadliest Killer Takes 12 Hours

**New first line** - hookscore **74 STRONG** (The Question):
> You have 12 hours. On the Oregon Trail, the thing that kills you most can take you before
> supper. What is it?

**Then:** 117.8-147.6 (Day 19 → "...all summer") + 150.4-153.2 ("Not snakes, not bandits, the
water."). Cut "That cup you drank from on day one? That's your answer." - it needs the long video.

**On-screen text, first 2 seconds:** `HEALTHY. THEN GONE.`

**Loop point:** last line "...the water." answers the first line's "What is it?" - on replay the
question lands with the viewer already knowing.

**Vertical framing:** the neighbour's three-panel sequence (crate → lying down → grave) must be
stacked or reframed per shot; in 16:9 the shovel and the wagon sit at the edges and will be cropped.
Centre the man's face and the tin cup. Grave-marker wide shot at the end: crop to the nearest
marker, not the whole trail.

**Then:** 24.6-51.6 (guidebook list → "You walk beside it every mile.").

**On-screen text, first 2 seconds:** `600 LB FLOUR`

**Loop point:** ends "You walk beside it every mile." → first line "...a two-thousand-mile walk" -

## Short C - The July 4th Deadline (24s)

**Title:** POV: Miss July 4th on the Oregon Trail and Winter Wins

**New first line** - hookscore **64 WORKABLE**:
> You have until July 4th to reach one rock in what's now Wyoming. Miss it, and the winter could
> kill you before Oregon.

**Then:** 51.6-69.3 ("You leave in spring for a reason." → "you're racing the winter.").

**On-screen text, first 2 seconds:** `ONE ROCK. ONE DATE.`

**Loop point:** ends "you're racing the winter" → first line "You have until July 4th..." - the
deadline restarts.

**Vertical framing:** the trail map unrolls horizontally in 16:9 - in 9:16 rotate it to run
bottom-to-top so the wagon icon climbs toward the "JULY 4" rock at the top. Independence Rock
needs its own vertical shot, rock filling the frame.

**Note:** weakest hook of the three (STAKES). If it underperforms A and B, rewrite the first line
before cutting more Shorts in this shape.
