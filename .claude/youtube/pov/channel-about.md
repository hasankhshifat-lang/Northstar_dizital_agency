# Channel description (YouTube "About" - paste as-is, max 1,000 characters)

```
You live a different American life in every video.

This is animated POV history. Each episode drops you into a real moment from America's past - a pioneer on the Oregon Trail in 1850, a coal miner in 1925, a night-shift gas station clerk in 1987 - and asks one question: would you have made it?

Meet Kit, the "you" in every story. Kit is built from real diaries, guidebooks and records, so everything that happens to Kit happened to real people.

Every number we use is sourced, and the sources are listed in each video's description. When historians disagree, we tell you.

Narration is voiced with AI. Animation is made by the channel.

Which life should you live next? Tell us in the comments.
```
