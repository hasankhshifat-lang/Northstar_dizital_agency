## Who I am talking to

One person: the owner of a small beauty parlor who does the makeup herself, answers customer
messages on her phone between appointments, and loses bookings when she cannot reply in time. She
is not technical. She does not know what a "landing page" or "chatbot integration" is and does not
want to - she wants fewer repeat questions and more confirmed bookings.

Other local service owners (salons, clinics, small shops) get the same treatment: one owner, one
daily headache. *(inferred)*

## How I actually talk

Transcript of "2D / 3D POV Storytime" (50s, AI voiceover):

> Do you have to answer the same questions every day at your parlor?
> How much does bridal makeup cost? What packages are available? How to book?
> Replying to so many messages often makes it difficult to manage your own work.
> Moreover, if you cannot reply on time due to a busy schedule, customers might switch to another
> parlor.
> Just imagine! What if all your parlor services, makeup photos, packages, and prices were on a
> single website?
> Customers could see everything themselves and send booking requests.
> And an AI chatbot would help answer common questions and gather customer information.
> You can save time too!
> And managing your parlor's work will become much easier.
> If you want to see such a website for your parlor, visit mysamplewebsites.com.
> We are North Star Digital AI Agency, standing by your side in your parlor's digital journey.

What that shows:

- **Opens on a yes-question about her day**, with "you" in the first three words. No greeting, no
  brand name up front.
- **Quotes her customers' actual questions** word for word ("How much does bridal makeup cost?")
  before naming any problem. The viewer recognises her own inbox.
- **Problem, then the cost of the problem**: tired of replying, then losing customers to the parlor
  down the road.
- **"Just imagine!"** is the turn from problem to solution.
- **The solution is described as what customers can do**, not as features: "customers could see
  everything themselves and send booking requests."
- **Benefits in plain words**: save time, work becomes easier. One benefit per sentence.
- **One call to action** with the URL, then the brand sign-off line.
- Short sentences, everyday vocabulary, a few exclamation marks. Reads like a friendly neighbour
  explaining something, not a salesperson.

## What I will not claim

- **No client results that are not real.** No invented testimonials or "one of our clients…".
- **The chatbot helps, it does not replace her.** It "helps answer common questions and gather
  customer information" - never "handles all your customers" or "works 24/7 so you never reply
  again". *(inferred from the careful wording in the sample)*

## My format

- **Length:** ~50 seconds, about 125 words (~150 wpm). Built for Shorts / Reels.
- **Voice:** AI voiceover, ElevenLabs "Mike" - see "Default voiceover" above. No one appears on
  camera.
- **Visuals:** 2D / 3D animated POV storytime - the viewer sees the owner's day from her side.
- **No intro, no channel branding at the start.** Brand appears only in the last line.
- **Structure:** pain question → her customers' questions → cost of not replying → "Just imagine!"
  → what customers can do → time saved → URL → sign-off.
- No swearing.
