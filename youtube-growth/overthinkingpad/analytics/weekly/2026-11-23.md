  embarrassing shirt) and a number twist (guessed half, real a quarter). The same study is doing well elsewhere this
  week ("Nadie te mira tanto como crees", 15.9k).
- **Weakest: b9ReSvwwjNw** (2 a.m. replay long): 5.75% viewed and 9.6% engaged. 101 of its 104 views are tagged
  SUBSCRIBER on a channel with 1 subscriber, which looks like an attribution anomaly. Likely reasons, in priority
  order: topic demand (the week's lowest topic score, 74, in a crowded niche), then the hook (drops at 20.8 s and
  27.7 s on the recap line and the "Here's the strange part" stall). These are hypotheses: about 10 real viewers.
- The full 7-question diagnoses are in `../../videos/b9ReSvwwjNw/performance.md`, `../../videos/VSaFr4doR0M/performance.md`
  and `../../videos/q4cK6ZazDEo/performance.md`. In short:
  - VSaFr4doR0M loses 36 pts at 12.6 s ("That feeling has a name.") and 14 pts at 170 s, where the second study
    repeats the design.
  - q4cK6ZazDEo has a weaker, more abstract premise than the shirt Short, with the same study-first hook.

## Single biggest weakness

**The long videos are not being discovered, and the few viewers they get leave in the first 15–30 s.**
- 138 long-form views against 1,331 Shorts views; no browse traffic; 5.8–16.8% viewed; 1 subscriber in total.
- The Shorts pull views (87% of traffic) but are not converting into long views or subscribers.
- Without impressions and CTR we can't tell whether topic demand or packaging is the cause of the low reach, so the
  first fix we can test is the opening (EXP-002). An owner Studio export would settle the rest.
