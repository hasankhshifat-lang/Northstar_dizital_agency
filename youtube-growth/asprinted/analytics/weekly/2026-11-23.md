## Single biggest weakness

**Long videos get almost no discovery: 42 views from 4 longs in a week (5.6% of channel views) vs 712 for the Shorts,
and only 28 related-video views flow from Shorts to longs.** By growth priority this is first a **topic-demand** problem:
the press-angle stories chosen (False Armistice, Twain, comet panic) have little long-form demand, while disaster and
frontier events with a "first reports vs the record" twist have large long-form demand right now (Custer 0.5–1.3M,
Galveston 49–376k on 18–66 min videos in the last 90 days; YouTube Data API search). Thumbnail was ruled out as the main
cause: custom thumbnails have been set since 11-17 and the longs did not improve (10 → 6 → 17 → 0). Hypothesis, not
proven (n=4, several things changed between episodes). Secondary: the three-day publishing gap (11-20…11-22).
