# Homepage

Scope: index.html (Persuade). Shared header, footer, type and colour also restyle services, projects, about and contact; their layouts stay.
Audience and action: a service-business owner on a phone (or an overseas owner at a desk) decides whether to trust Northstar and starts a conversation via the form or WhatsApp.
Proof on hand: published BDT packages, four portfolio sites (two labelled demo or concept), the live ElevenLabs assistant. No testimonials or founder material yet; do not add placeholders.
Constraints: English only; the example AI conversation is labelled as an example; no reply-time or payment-term claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A precise, light studio site that shows the offer instead of describing it. It refuses the dark neon "AI agency" page: no glow, gradient text, 3D face or buzzword ticker.
OWN-WORLD: Cool near-white ground, near-black ink, one logo-violet accent used for actions and the "recommended" mark, one deep ink-violet closing panel. Schibsted Grotesk set tight for display and body (replaced Geist after the overused-font detector finding), Spline Sans Mono for metadata: payment terms, durations, frame URLs, labels and tags, step numerals and the example label. Prices are set in the sans with tabular figures. The close is an inset ink-violet panel, not a full-bleed field. Hairline rules, 12px radii, real screenshots in thin browser frames, authored SVG icons.
STORY: The visitor learns in one line what is sold (websites plus AI receptionists for service businesses), sees the starting price and delivery time, watches an example booking happen, compares three fixed packages, sees real work, and starts a project or WhatsApp chat.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: H1 at about 4.5rem, one sentence of subcopy, a mono price-and-time line, "Start a project" (ink) and "Chat on WhatsApp" (outline) side by side. Right: the bridal site screenshot in a browser frame, overlapped at bottom-left by a phone-width example chat card. On 390px the H1, subcopy, price line and both buttons fit above the fold; the visual follows.
