## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary:** owners of small service businesses in Bangladesh (salons and bridal studios, clinics and doctors, cleaning companies, restaurants, contractors) who need a credible website that brings in enquiries. They often evaluate on a phone and prefer to make contact over WhatsApp.
- **Secondary:** independent professionals and personal brands wanting a portfolio site.
- **Growing audience:** overseas clients. The site currently prices only in BDT; how international buyers are priced and addressed is an open decision.

The visitor's job: decide whether Northstar can be trusted to build their site (and optionally an AI assistant), understand roughly what it costs, and start a conversation.

## Product Purpose
Northstar Dizital AI is a small web and AI studio. It designs and builds business websites, landing pages, e-commerce sites and UI/UX, and adds AI where it genuinely saves time: AI voice assistants that answer calls and book appointments, chat responses, and AI-assisted content. Success is a qualified enquiry through the contact form or WhatsApp.

## Positioning
All four were confirmed as differentiators:
1. **AI voice and chat agents** for small businesses, which typical local web designers do not offer.
2. **Premium design quality** above the usual local business site.
3. **Fast, fixed, published prices**: Starter ৳8,000–12,000 (3–5 days), Business ৳18,000–30,000 (7–12 days), Premium AI ৳35,000–60,000+ (2–4 weeks). Third-party AI usage fees and advanced integrations are quoted separately.
4. **Honest, human-reviewed work**: scope and timeline confirmed before work begins, AI output reviewed by a person, sample and concept work labelled as such.

## Operating Context
- Enquiries arrive through the contact form (`contact.html`: name, email, business, service, budget range in BDT, preferred contact method) or WhatsApp (+8801872692783), email contact@hasankhshifat.com.
- Workflow sold to clients: Discovery → Design → Development → Launch.
- Clients supply business details, services, contact/booking method, brand assets, and real photos or copy.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS site: `index.html`, `about.html`, `services.html`, `projects.html`, `contact.html`, shared `style.css` and `script.js` (header/footer injected by script). Production: https://samplewebsite.tech/
- AI voice assistant capabilities depend on the client's systems and approved integrations; never promise more than that.
- **Open:** pricing and copy for overseas clients (currency, English-first positioning).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: **Northstar Dizital AI** (spelling "Dizital" is intentional). Logo at `assets/logo.png`.
- Voice: plain, practical, honest, no hype. "AI applied where it genuinely helps." Replies promise "a practical next step".

## Evidence on Hand
- Portfolio (`assets/projects/`): Personal Business Portfolio, Bridal Studio, Doctor Portfolio (demo, sample content), Cleaning Business Concept (concept). Labels for demo/concept must stay.
- The user confirmed: some projects are live sites for paying clients; real client testimonials exist; the founder is willing to be shown by name and photo. **None of these are in the repo yet.** Use only the exact quotes, names, photos and client details the user supplies; never write placeholder testimonials or guess which projects are paid.
- No client logos, metrics, awards or press. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Every page earns an enquiry: the path to WhatsApp or the form is always obvious.
2. Show, don't claim: real work and real words beat adjectives.
3. Be honest about scope, price and what AI can do.
4. The site itself is the portfolio piece; its craft is part of the pitch.
5. Mobile-first, because the buyer is usually on a phone.
